Missed weeks

Each calling now carries one emergency skip. Miss a week and it covers it: the run waits a week instead of starting over, and the skip comes back when the run is done. The first deed of the kind after a missed week gives a short buff that lasts twice as long.

In the run with one bad week (w/c 23 Nov), the sparring partner moved in —. Under 2.4's rule: —.

Why: missing one opportunity didn't materially affect habit formation (Lally et al. 2010, Eur J Soc Psychol 40:998). Goals with a costly "emergency reserve" beat easy and hard goals for persistence, including after a failed week (Sharif & Shu 2017, JMR 54:495; 2021, OBHDP 163:17). Broken streaks cut later engagement, less so when they can be repaired (Silverman & Barasch 2023, JCR 49:1095). And of 54 interventions in a gym megastudy of over 60,000 people, a small bonus for coming back after a missed workout did best (Milkman et al. 2021, Nature 600:478).

Weapon arts and the fountain still lean one way (right about 94–96% of the time) but each loses in real cases, so I left them as choices.

Profiling it on your A54

  1. On the phone: Settings → About phone → Software information, tap Build number seven times. Then Developer options → USB debugging on.
  2. Install 2.5, plug the phone into your PC and allow the computer when the phone asks.
  3. On the PC, open Chrome at chrome://inspect, tick Discover USB devices, start a delve on the phone, and click inspect under the Grindstone WebView.
  4. Performance tab → record while the crawl plays a fight → stop. Frames over the budget show red. In the Console, Crawl.stats() gives the draw cost and frame gaps of the last 600 frames.
  5. Turn USB debugging off afterwards: Grindstone only opens to DevTools while it's on.

What to expect: the A54's screen runs at 120 Hz, so the budget is 8.3 ms a frame (16.7 ms at 60 Hz). The crawl's own work is under 2 ms a frame in the middle of the range here, so walking should hold 120; the slowest frames in busy fights may drop to 60. I couldn't confirm the WebView ticks at 120 rather than 60 on your phone: Crawl.stats() will say (a gap near 8 ms is 120).

Method and what's left out

The model player. Opens every chest, puts half his gold into coffers, wears the best kit on paper, tries each companion and keeps the best run, pushes on while runs clear, and runs once each evening.

Effort levels. Ordinary: 2 strength, 2 endurance, 2 study, looking after yourself 6 days. Strong: 3, 3, 4, 7. Lapsed: 0, 1, 0, 3. Your logged pace: 1, 1, 0, 7. Chores and feeding the birds run alongside each.

Left out. Chores, birds and study before 8 Nov (no app backup to read), anything after 17 Dec on the real roster (the 12-week targets repeat the roster), and how the hero looked or what he wore before.
